Frequently asked questions How do I create a house? Open the app, sign up, and tap "Create a house." You'll get an invite code to share with your housemates so they can join the same house. How do I invite housemates? Go to your Profile and share your house invite code (there's a Share button). Housemates enter that code when they join a house. How are points decided? Each chore is worth points based on how big the job is — tougher or longer chores (like cleaning the oven) are worth more than quick ones (like taking out the bins). You can adjust any chore's points in the Chore Bank. Can I use it on both iPhone and Android? Yes — housemates on different devices can share the same house using the same invite code. How do I turn off chore photos? Go to Profile → Appearance → "Show chore photos" to hide photos. You can also report a photo by opening it and tapping Report. How do I delete my account? Go to Profile → "Delete my account." This permanently removes your account and your data. A housemate posted something inappropriate — what do I do? Open the photo and tap Report, or turn photos off in Profile. You can also email us at fluxdevelopmentsinc@gmail.com and we'll act on it.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 June 2026

CleanMates ("we", "us", or "our") operates the CleanMates mobile application (the "App"). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use CleanMates:

  • Account information — your email address and display name when you create an account.
  • House & chore data — house name, invite codes, chore assignments, and completion logs (including optional photos you choose to upload as proof).
  • Postcode — your UK postcode, used only to verify you are in a supported region.
  • Energy lead information — if you submit a request via the Move In tab, we collect the details you provide (e.g. name, contact details, postcode) in order to refer you to our energy partner.
  • Device & usage data — basic technical information (e.g. device type, OS version, app version) collected automatically to maintain the service.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and operate the CleanMates app and chore rota features.
  • Allow housemates to join shared houses and track chore completion.
  • Display leaderboard points within your house group.
  • Process and forward energy referral requests to our partner (Do Energy / Student Energy Group) when you opt in via the Move In tab.
  • Improve and debug the app.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only in these circumstances:

  • Housemates — your display name, chore completions, and points are visible to other members of your shared house within the app.
  • Energy partner — if you submit an energy referral, your submitted details are shared with Do Energy / Student Energy Group solely to process that referral.
  • Service providers — we use Supabase (database and authentication infrastructure). Your data is stored on Supabase servers in accordance with their privacy policy.
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose data if required by law.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely via Supabase. Session tokens are stored locally on your device using encrypted storage. We apply row-level security policies to ensure users can only access data belonging to their own house.

5. Photo Uploads

Photos you upload as chore completion proof are stored on our servers and are visible to members of your house. Do not upload photos containing sensitive personal information. You can delete your chore logs (and associated photos) at any time from within the app.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data will be deleted within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it by law.

7. Your Rights (UK GDPR)

As a UK resident, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Object to or restrict how we process your data.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

8. Children's Privacy

CleanMates is not intended for anyone under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or email. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
